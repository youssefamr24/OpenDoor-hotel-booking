Description

Comfortable Accommodations: Triple garden view hotel in Cairo offers family rooms with air conditioning, private bathroom, and city views. Each room includes a work desk, dining table, and free WiFi.

Dining and Leisure: Guests can enjoy a traditional restaurant serving American cuisine and a sun terrace. Additional facilities include a hot tub, outdoor seating area, and a shared kitchen.

Convenient Location: Located 10 minutes from Tahrir Square and 0.6 miles from the Egyptian Museum, the hotel is 11 miles from Cairo International Airport. Nearby attractions include Cairo Tower and the Giza Pyramids.
